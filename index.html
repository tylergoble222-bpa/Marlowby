Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is Marlowby?

Marlowby is a proposed subscription-box service featuring products and stories from a different U.S. city each month.

What might be inside a box?

A curated selection of locally made goods, which could include food items, home goods, art, stationery, or other products reflecting the featured city.

Why focus on small businesses?

Independent makers help give communities their character. Featuring them can introduce customers to businesses and products they might not otherwise discover.

Are the prices and makers real?

No. Prices, sample makers, and products on this student-created BPA website are illustrative examples.
